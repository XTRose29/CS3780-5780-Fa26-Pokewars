# Acknowledgments and image credits

Thank you to the original Pokémon artists, designers, render artists, and creative teams whose artwork underlies these silhouettes. We appreciate their work and do not claim to have created the underlying artwork or character designs.

Thank you to the [PokéAPI data contributors](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi) and [PokéAPI sprites contributors](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites) for collecting, organizing, preserving, and sharing the source assets.

[Image credits](data/image_credits.csv) cover every one of the 3,172 silhouettes, with its source URL, checksums, project acknowledgment, rights notice, modifications, and thanks. [Source credits](data/source_credits.csv) cover all 3,187 selected source entries, including duplicate aliases. [Preview credits](data/preview_credits.csv) connect every panel of the three preview sheets to its image credit.

The [pinned upstream README](data/attribution_sources/pokeapi_sprites_README.md) preserves the upstream project's acknowledgments and links in their original context. It also discusses collections not used in this curated dataset.

Individual original artists have not been verified for these files. Entries explicitly record `individual_artist_not_verified`; a source repository contributor or rights holder is not substituted for an unidentified individual author. We thank the original creators while preserving this identification gap.

These silhouettes are derived from existing artwork by alpha-mask extraction, resizing, and padding. The upstream notice attributes image copyright to The Pokémon Company. The [original notices](licenses/) are preserved; their presence and these acknowledgments do not grant new rights, constitute permission, or establish fair use. The intended use is a nonprofit school-course competition. This project is independent and not endorsed by the source projects or rights holders.
