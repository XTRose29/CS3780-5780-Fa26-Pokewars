# Attribution

The images are newly augmented silhouettes derived from the original Pokémon
artwork collected by PokéAPI contributors. Thank you to the original artists,
designers, and contributors. Pokémon artwork and character designs belong to
their respective rights holders, including The Pokémon Company.

The original [acknowledgments](ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md),
[dataset acknowledgment](ORIGINAL_ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md), and preserved notices
in `POKEAPI.txt` and `SPRITES.txt` accompany this student package. Relative
`data/` and `licenses/` links inside historical notices refer to the upstream
project. Individual original artists are unverified where the source says so.

Full source URLs and per-source credit records are preserved in
`organizer/generation/upstream/data/image_credits.csv`; the organizer's
`image_audit.csv.gz` links each generated image to its source variant. Those
links are organizer-only because they would disclose evaluation identities.
General source acknowledgment and notices remain available to students.

Changes in this release include mirroring, rotation, scaling, cropping, bounded occlusion,
background shading, blur, contrast changes, noise, and 32×32 grayscale output.
Attribution does not grant new rights or imply endorsement.
